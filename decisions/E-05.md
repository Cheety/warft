# E-05 — Class mix and constants

**Status:** ruled · **Source:** panel E-05 of the architecture overview, revision 2 (`workpod-plattform-leitstand.html`), carried over per AP-0.1. Translated under [the language ruling](language.md); the identifiers are untouched.

## Ruling

The five constants are planning values of the occupancy table, not operating values. Admission and preemption decide exclusively on PSI and measured peak RSS (R-C). The mix behind the given values is 20/30/35/15 across tiny/small/medium/large.

## Rationale

1. **This bounds the risk of wrong constants completely:** the table plans imprecisely. It cannot overbook the machine, because the runtime does not read it. That is the real conclusion of this point — not the number, but the place where it takes effect.
2. **The mix adds up:** 0.2·0.1 + 0.3·0.3 + 0.35·1.0 + 0.15·2.0 = 0.76 cores and 0.2·128 + 0.3·384 + 0.35·1024 + 0.15·3072 = 960 MB. The table's given values therefore describe a build-heavy operation — that had not been said anywhere so far and is the actual finding.
3. **A read- and plan-heavy mix (30/40/25/5)** yields 0.5 cores and 600 MB and thus about half as many slots again. On a platform whose everyday work is reading, planning and summarizing, the mix will move in that direction — the given value is deliberately the pessimistic one.
4. **Conservative is the right direction here.** Too few planned slots cost money. Too many cost the evidence from R-C, and that is more expensive.
