# E-04 — Cell size

**Status:** ruled · **Source:** panel E-04 of the architecture overview, revision 2 (`workpod-plattform-leitstand.html`), carried over per AP-0.1. Translated under [the language ruling](language.md); the identifiers are untouched.

## Ruling

The cell boundary is the smaller of 500 active projects and the node count at which p99 of `admitted → occupied` reaches 2 seconds with free slots. Starting value 32 work nodes. A new cell is opened at 80 %, not at 100 %.

## Rationale

1. **The second number is not guessed, it is our own.** 2 seconds is the waiting budget of interactive jobs from R-B. A cell is full exactly when its admission starts to break the platform's promise — not when the database sweats. The scaling limit is thereby measured against the user's experience instead of against a utilization gauge, and that is the same distinction R-C makes for memory.
2. **The first number comes from the radius:** 500 projects is the order of magnitude that can still be named and notified during a disruption. It will almost always be the smaller one — exactly as V-03 predicts, and exactly why it stands first.
3. **80 % as the trigger,** because splitting a cell takes days and the load arrives in jumps: a single new project brings two hundred subjobs with it.
4. **The technical ceiling lies far above that** — a Postgres primary carries the write rates of a cell with ease. Whoever looks for that ceiling first builds cells too large and gets an incident nobody can explain afterwards.

## Overturned by

a measurement that shows p99 under 2 seconds even with considerably more nodes. Then the second number rises — the first one never.
