# E-03 — Cell cut

**Status:** ruled · **Source:** panel E-03 of the architecture overview, revision 2 (`workpod-plattform-leitstand.html`), carried over per AP-0.1. Translated under [the language ruling](language.md); the identifiers are untouched.

## Ruling

The tenant cuts the cell, the repository family cuts the locality group inside it. The two candidates lie on different layers — data sovereignty and incident radius are cell properties, cache warmth is a node property.

## Rationale

1. **A tenant never crosses a cell boundary.** If it grows too large it gets several cells — and those are then cut by repository family. The rule stays the same, it applies one layer down.
2. **A project lies wholly in one cell** (V-03). That is the sentence everything else follows from, and it is not softened.
3. **Regulatory requirements get a cell of their own, not a setting of their own.** A setting that promises separation is one configuration error away from no separation.

## Overturned by

Nothing — the case that seems to break the rule is already contained in it.
