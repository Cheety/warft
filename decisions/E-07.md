# E-07 — Regions and retention

**Status:** ruled · **Source:** panel E-07 of the architecture overview, revision 2 (`workpod-plattform-leitstand.html`), carried over per AP-0.1. Translated under [the language ruling](language.md); the identifiers are untouched.

## Ruling

One region per tenant, default the EU. The global layer holds identifiers only — external→principal, project→cell, image index, cost accounts. Content never leaves its cell.

## Rationale

1. **The second ruling carries the first.** Because no content lies globally (V-03), worldwide operation is possible without a single record leaving its region. Without that property every cell assignment becomes a transmission.
2. **Deletion is a job with a 30-day deadline** and executable, because every object carries its project (K-01). What stays in the audit trail is the deletion receipt, not the content.
3. **Statutory retention obligations are set by the tenant.** That is a legal question of their house; the architecture answers it with “settable per tenant” instead of with a number — and makes the setting a cell property so that it cannot be circumvented.

## Overturned by

a requirement that demands content in the global layer. Then the global layer falls, not the period.
