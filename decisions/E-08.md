# E-08 — The duty officer

**Status:** ruled · **Source:** panel E-08 of the architecture overview, revision 2 (`workpod-plattform-leitstand.html`), carried over per AP-0.1. Translated under [the language ruling](language.md); the identifiers are untouched.

## Ruling

A role on call, at least two trained people, one of them active. It may halt alone and never widen alone. The halt is a state with an expiry, not a command with an effect.

## Rationale

1. **The expiry after 60 minutes is the most important part.** A forgotten halt is otherwise the second incident of the day. A state that needs attention in order to persist disappears by itself as soon as nobody is looking any more — and that is exactly the right way round.
2. **The halt has a second path:** a field in admission and a file on the control node that is read at every admission decision. That way it also takes effect when the API no longer answers — the case in which you need it.
3. **Halting is unilateral, widening never.** That is the only asymmetry an operations role needs: the safe direction may be fast, the unsafe one may not.
4. **Two people are the minimum, not the goal.** A role with one human is not a role but a holiday from operations.

## Overturned by

Nothing. the number of people grows with the fleet, the rule does not.
